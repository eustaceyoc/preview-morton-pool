# Morton Pool Solutions: sample site

A one-page sample site I put together for Morton Pool Solutions (Wylie, TX). It's a concept, not their official website, and it says so at the top of the page and in the footer.

Live preview: https://preview-morton-pool.vercel.app

The request form doesn't send anything to a server. It just opens your own email app with a message addressed to mortonpoolsolutions@gmail.com, and the phone buttons dial (469) 571-9608. There's no tracking or analytics, and the fonts are self-hosted.

## What's on the page

Only details from Morton Pool Solutions's own website and public listings: business name, owners Rick and Susan Morton, the Wylie address, phone, email, the services and service areas, the no-repair note on equipment, the free estimates and financing, and the pricing line, all from mortonpoolsolutions.com. Anything I couldn't confirm is left out or marked to be confirmed. There are no reviews, ratings, prices, years in business or team photos.

The page has `noindex, nofollow` in a meta tag and in the `X-Robots-Tag` header (`vercel.json`), and `robots.txt` disallows everything, so it shouldn't turn up in search.
